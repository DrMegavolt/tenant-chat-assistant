# Clearview Property Care Financing Options

## Overview

Clearview Property Care may offer financing for qualifying property-care projects. Financing is handled through third-party lending partners and is subject to application approval. Clearview can help customers understand available plans, but only the lender can approve credit and final terms.

## Qualifying Services

Financing may be available for larger HVAC, electrical, and exterior maintenance projects. Routine window cleaning, small hourly jobs, and low-cost maintenance visits are usually paid directly unless they are part of a larger approved project.

## Payment Plans

Customers may ask whether they can pay over time. The approved response is that payment plans may be available for qualifying projects, and the team can confirm current options after reviewing the requested service and estimated project amount. Exact monthly payment amounts should not be quoted by the assistant.

## Promotional Terms

Promotional financing, if available, may include limited-time terms. Promotional terms can expire or change. Customers should review lender disclosures before accepting an offer. The assistant should not claim that a promotion is currently active unless the promotion is present in current approved documents.

## Follow-Up

If a customer wants to discuss financing, collect their name, contact information, service type, address or ZIP code, and a short project summary. Create a follow-up lead for the office team to confirm current financing options.
